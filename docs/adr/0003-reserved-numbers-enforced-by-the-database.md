# The database, not the forms, keeps Registrations off Reserved numbers

A Reserved number is never a Registration's number. D1 itself enforces this: it refuses to store a Registration on a Reserved number, and refuses to reserve a number a Registration still has. Several people write at once on race day, typically two or three Admins at the Race desk plus Participants registering from their phones. A check in a form can pass for two requests before either is stored. Examples: an Admin moves a Registration to 13 while another reserves 13, or a Participant registers just as their number is being reserved. Form checks would let these overlap silently. With the rule in the database, the losing write fails instead: the Admin sees an error, or the reservation is retried or refused as a whole. The forms still check first, so Admins get a proper German message in the normal case. The database rule is the backstop, and keeping it means every new way of writing numbers has to respect it too.
